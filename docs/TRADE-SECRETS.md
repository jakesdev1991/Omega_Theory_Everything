# Trade-secret and confidential-information handling

This repository is public. Anything committed here—including issues, pull requests, releases, and Git history—should be treated as disclosed to the public. A later deletion or repository-visibility change does not reliably undo disclosure. Publicly disclosed blueprint or whitepaper details should not be treated as secret merely because this licensing policy reserves copyright in their expression.

## Keep out of this repository

Do not commit credentials, signing keys, API tokens, private encryption material, personal or sensitive data, non-public economic parameters, unreleased security controls, anti-abuse/collusion-detection rules, private datasets, or confidential agent-routing and deployment details. Use an access-controlled private system with least-privilege access, documented owners, secure key management, and appropriate confidentiality agreements.

## If sensitive material is exposed

Assume it may have been copied. Revoke or rotate credentials immediately, assess affected people and systems, follow any applicable incident-response and reporting duties, and then remove the material from current files and history where feasible. History cleanup is not a substitute for revocation or notification.

## Separate legal rights

Trade-secret protection generally depends on information remaining secret and reasonable measures being taken to protect it; this note cannot restore secrecy to public information or guarantee a legal result. Use counsel to determine what qualifies and how to protect it. The source-available/proprietary notices in this repository do not make publicly disclosed information confidential.
