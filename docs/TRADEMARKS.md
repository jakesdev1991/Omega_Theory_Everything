- Search relevant trademark registries and markets before adopting or claiming a mark.

- Use `™` only as an unregistered claim and never use `®` unless the specific mark is actually registered for the relevant goods/services.
- Keep records of first use and specimen evidence; review third-party names and possible conflicts before launch.
