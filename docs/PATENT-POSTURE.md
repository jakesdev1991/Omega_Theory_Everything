# Patent posture and disclosure note

> This is a factual-status notice and preservation checklist, not patent advice.

## Status represented by this repository

No patent application, pending application, issued patent, or patent ownership record has been verified for this project as part of this change. Accordingly, this repository **does not claim that any invention is patented or patent-pending**. Do not use “patent pending” or make a novelty, validity, or exclusivity claim unless a patent attorney verifies the facts and authorizes the wording.

The project is already publicly accessible on GitHub, including scientific materials, whitepapers, and product-design documents. The local checkout is shallow, so it is not sufficient to establish the first-public-disclosure date of each feature or document. Public disclosure may affect patent rights; timing rules and consequences vary by jurisdiction and facts, and removal of a public file does not erase prior disclosure.

## License interaction

Apache-2.0 applies to the scientific paths listed in the root [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE). Section 3 contains a limited, express patent license from each contributor for patent claims that contributor can license and that are necessarily infringed by the contribution alone or by its combination with the covered work. It also contains a patent-litigation termination provision. Have counsel assess how that grant interacts with any present or future patent strategy before accepting contributions or making filings.

The all-rights-reserved product notice is not a patent filing and does not create a patent right. Copyright restrictions on expression do not by themselves create exclusive rights in an abstract system, method, or idea.

## Recommended next steps

1. Before further public disclosure, have a patent attorney review the actual proposed claims, ownership, prior art, and all disclosure dates.
2. Preserve an evidence log of repository commits, pull requests, releases, talks, posts, demos, papers, and other disclosures. Reconstruct missing history from GitHub and participant records; do not assume this shallow checkout is complete.
3. Confirm the inventors and ownership chain for any potentially patentable technical invention and resolve contractor, employer, and contributor rights.
4. Do not describe an application as filed or pending unless counsel has verified the application number, filing date, inventor list, and permitted public wording.

The steps above are operational hygiene only; they do not establish patentability, priority, or ownership.
