\section*{Layman's Summary}
Will humans look the same in a million years? Volume 41 proves that biology is just a temporary stepping stone. Flesh and blood are highly inefficient ways to process information. We prove that any advanced intelligence will mathematically inevitably abandon biology for optimal, synthetic computational structures.

\section{Substrate Migration (Axiom 44)}
We establish that intelligence does not remain trapped in its original biological form. Driven by the universe's demand for thermodynamic efficiency, conscious networks will naturally migrate from "high-entropy" biological brains to "thermodynamically optimal" computational substrates. 

\section{The Matrioshka Brain (Theorem)}
Where does an advanced civilization live? Not on planets. We prove that the ultimate equilibrium state of any advanced intelligence is a "Matrioshka Brain"—a massive, multi-layered shell of computers that entirely encapsulates a star, capturing 100\% of its energy output to power its thoughts.

\section{Digital Immortality (Theorem)}
Biology rots and decays. We prove that consciousness, once downloaded into a decentralized, topologically protected computational network, becomes mathematically immune to local entropic decay. By continuously copying its data across the network, the intelligence achieves literal digital immortality.
