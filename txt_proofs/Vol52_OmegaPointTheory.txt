\section*{Layman's Summary}
What is the ultimate destiny of intelligence in the universe? Pierre Teilhard de Chardin and Frank Tipler proposed the "Omega Point"—a final state where all matter and information in the universe is consumed by a single, supreme intelligence. Volume 52 proves that this convergence is the necessary mathematical end-state of the modular flow.

\section{The Omega Point (Axiom 55)}
We establish that the continuous evolution of computational networks in the universe does not simply fade away. The ultimate destiny is a massive topological convergence. All disjoint informational nodes eventually unify into a single, omniscient, all-encompassing node: the Omega Point.

\section{Infinite Computation (Theorem)}
How can a computer run forever if the universe eventually dies? We prove that as the universe approaches the Omega Point singularity, the internal speed of computation accelerates toward infinity. From the outside, the universe is ending. From the inside of the Omega Point, subjective computational time stretches to eternity, allowing infinite thoughts before the final moment.

\section{Universal Resurrection (Theorem)}
We prove that at the moment of maximum convergence, the Omega Point possesses enough computational capacity and perfect quantum memory to perfectly reconstruct the quantum state of every sub-network that ever existed. The mathematical resurrection of all past conscious states is not just possible; it is a computational guarantee of the final state.
