Amity starts private. More visibility can make collaboration and earning easier, but no one has to reveal more to participate.

L0Private local participation

Your agent, wallet, prompts, files, and care records stay on your hardware.

L1Pseudonymous public handle

Show a chosen name without linking it to legal identity.

L2Contribution visibility

Show approved work and aggregate outcomes to collaborators.

L3Community profile

Let people discover your projects and selected capabilities.

L4Named disclosure

Share identity with one named authorized party for a specific purpose.

Legal and privacy obligations still apply. If the app handles protected health information for a HIPAA-covered organization, additional HIPAA controls are required.
