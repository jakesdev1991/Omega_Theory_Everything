\section*{Layman's Summary}
Speculation about the long-term future of intelligence includes the idea that it may move from biological to synthetic substrates. This volume records a toy finite model of substrate costs. Its numbers are chosen, not measured, and it says nothing about what will happen.

\section{Substrate Transition Costs (Stipulated Model)}
The file defines a three-substrate cost table and proves the metric properties of that table. The values are stipulated. The file does not justify them from physics or engineering.

\section{Hybrid Substrate as Cheapest Bridge (Formal Result within the Model)}
Under the stipulated table, the hybrid substrate gives the cheapest path between the other two. This is a finite check of that table.

\section{Substrate Migration (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that intelligence inevitably migrates from biological brains to optimal computational structures has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn as a claim of this work and is listed as an open item.

\section{Matrioshka Brains and Digital Immortality (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claims about Matrioshka brains as equilibrium states, and about immortality through distributed copying, have no formal counterpart. They are withdrawn.
