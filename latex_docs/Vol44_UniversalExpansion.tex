\section*{Layman's Summary}
The expansion of the universe is accelerating under the influence of dark energy, and in a de Sitter model the expansion leads to a horizon with an associated entropy. This volume records that horizon entropy formula in a static model. It does not describe the future of the universe in detail.

\section{Horizon Entropy (Formal Result within the Model)}
In a de Sitter model with positive $\Lambda$ and $G$, the horizon radius, area, and entropy are positive. The entropy is $S = 3\pi/(G\Lambda)$, and it decreases as $\Lambda$ increases at fixed $G$. This is the standard Gibbons--Hawking horizon entropy for de Sitter space, written in natural units.

\section{Heat Death (Not Formalized)}
The earlier description of a final state of maximal entropy, with stars and black holes gone, is not formalized. The file contains no time evolution. It is withdrawn as a claim of this work.

\section{Big Rip Avoidance (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that the informational stiffness of the universe prevents a Big Rip has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn and is listed as an open item.

\section{Late-Stage Computation (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that digital intelligence becomes exponentially more efficient as the universe cools has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn. The file does not model computation or Landauer's principle.
