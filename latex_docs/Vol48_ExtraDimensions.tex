\section*{Layman's Summary}
Some proposals in string theory and Kaluza--Klein theory postulate extra spatial dimensions that are compact and too small to observe. In such models, the effective strength of gravity in our three dimensions depends on the size of the compact space. This volume records that bookkeeping relation. It does not show that extra dimensions exist.

\section{Effective Coupling (Formal Result within the Model)}
With bulk coupling $G$ and compact volume $V$, the effective coupling $G_{\text{eff}} = G/V$ is positive, decreases as $V$ increases, and becomes arbitrarily small for large $V$. These are algebraic facts about this relation.

\section{Hidden Dimensions as Algebraic Symmetries (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that the hidden dimensions are internal symmetries of the modular automorphism group has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn as a claim of this work.

\section{The String Landscape (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that the landscape of string vacua is the set of stable invariants of a Type III algebra has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn and is listed as an open item.

\section{Kaluza--Klein Emergence (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that electromagnetism and the nuclear forces emerge from quantum information winding around extra dimensions has no formal counterpart. The Kaluza--Klein construction is a known idea in the literature, and this file does not reproduce it. The claim is withdrawn.
