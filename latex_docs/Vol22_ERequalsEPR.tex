\section*{Layman's Summary}
ER = EPR is a conjecture that quantum entanglement and wormhole-like spacetime connections are two descriptions of the same structure. This volume records a model in which these notions are defined through mutual information. It does not prove the conjecture for physical spacetimes.

\section{ER = EPR (Conjecture, Model Correspondence Only)}
The conjecture is stated in the formal file as a correspondence inside the model. It is not established for physical spacetimes or for general quantum states. The earlier claim that this identity is an axiom that unifies quantum mechanics and general relativity is withdrawn.

\section{Entanglement and Geometry in the Model (Formal Result within the Model)}
Within the model, the distance between two regions is defined through how much information they fail to share. The formal file shows, for example, that perfect overlap implies zero distance, and that the absence of entanglement implies the absence of an ER bridge. These are consequences of the definitions in the file. They are not a derivation of spacetime geometry from quantum information.

\section{Wormhole Traversability and Teleportation (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that traversable wormholes require negative informational pressure, and that this requirement coincides with the quantum teleportation protocol, has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn as a claim of this work and is listed as an open item.

\section{Scope and Open Items}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Not established here: ER = EPR for physical spacetimes; traversable wormholes; the relation of the model's distance to metric distance in general relativity.
\end{itemize}
