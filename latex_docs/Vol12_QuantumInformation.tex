\section*{Layman's Summary}
Quantum information has rules that restrict copying and transmitting states. This volume states a small two-amplitude model of those restrictions. The model captures one algebraic form of the no-cloning obstruction and a capacity bound. It is not a derivation of quantum mechanics from the Omega framework.

\section{No-Cloning Obstruction (Formal Result within the Model)}
The formal file shows that a normalized nonorthogonal pair of states witnesses the failure of the algebraic copying condition for all normalized states. The copying condition is supplied as a hypothesis. This does not derive the no-cloning theorem from modular flow or from unitarity of a physical process.

\section{Capacity and Bell-Pair Entropy (Formal Result within the Model)}
The formal file records a capacity bound, and shows that the entropy of a Bell pair in the model lies below that capacity. These are finite statements about the model's definitions.

\section{Conservation of Entropy (Model Assumption)}
The conservation of entropy is stated as a model assumption. It is not derived in this volume. The earlier phrasing ``the universe never forgets'' is withdrawn as a physical claim.

\section{Entanglement Routing and Teleportation (No Formal Counterpart)}
Earlier descriptions of entanglement routing and teleportation as consequences of the framework have no formal counterpart here. They are withdrawn as claims of this work.

\section{Scope and Open Items}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Not established here: no-cloning in the standard tensor-product setting; derivation of the copying condition; teleportation protocols; a physical interpretation of the capacity.
\end{itemize}
