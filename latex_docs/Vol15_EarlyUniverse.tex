\section*{Layman's Summary}
Cosmic inflation is a period of accelerated expansion in the very early universe. It is usually modeled by a fluid or scalar field whose pressure is strongly negative relative to its energy density. This volume records that standard mathematical condition and a de Sitter benchmark. It does not explain the origin of inflation.

\section{Initial State (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier description of the universe beginning as a state of maximal informational density and zero topological complexity has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn as a claim of this work and is listed as an open item.

\section{Cosmic Inflation (Formal Result within the Model)}
For an equation of state $w < -1/3$, the formal file shows that $\rho + 3p < 0$, and hence that the acceleration source is positive. A de Sitter vacuum with $w = -1$ and positive energy density is a concrete realization of this condition. This is standard cosmological algebra. The earlier claim that inflation was driven by a transition from a longitudinal to a transverse informational state is withdrawn; the file contains no such transition.

\section{Primordial Fluctuations (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that quantum jitters in modular flow seeded the cosmic web has no formal counterpart. The file does not compute a power spectrum. It is withdrawn as a claim of this work and is listed as an open item.
