\section*{Layman's Summary}
Bell's theorem shows that no local hidden-variable theory reproduces all quantum correlations. The CHSH inequality is a standard test of this. This volume records the numerical facts about the CHSH bound and the singlet state. It is a check of standard quantum mechanics, not a new theory of non-locality.

\section{CHSH Violation (Formal Result)}
The classical CHSH bound is $2$. The Tsirelson bound $2\sqrt{2}$ is strictly larger, and the singlet state reaches it. These are standard results, and the formal file verifies them.

\section{Local Realism (Model Assumption Statement)}
The local-realism bound is used in the file as the classical bound. The earlier claim that local realism is prohibited by the non-commutative structure of the framework's algebra is not formalized. It is withdrawn as a claim of this work.

\section{Contextuality (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claims about Kochen--Specker contextuality, and about the outcome of measurements depending on the full experimental context, are not formalized. They are withdrawn as claims of this work and are listed as open items.

\section{Action at a Distance and Causality (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that causality is preserved because non-local influences propagate through an ``Archive Mode'' has no formal counterpart. The file contains no dynamics, no signaling analysis, and no ``Archive Mode''. It is withdrawn.
