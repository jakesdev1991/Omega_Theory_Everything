\section*{Layman's Summary}
Galactic-scale civilizations are a speculative topic. This volume records a small graph model of stars and travel times, and two basic properties of it. It does not describe any real or possible civilization.

\section{Galactic Adjacency (Formal Result within the Model)}
In the model, the adjacency relation is symmetric, and no star links to itself. These follow from the stated axioms of the model.

\section{Travel Time (Formal Result within the Model)}
Travel time is distance divided by speed. It is monotone in distance. This is elementary.

\section{Supermassive Black Holes as Computers (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that supermassive black holes serve as central computing nodes for a galactic civilization has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn.

\section{Penrose Process (No Formal Counterpart)}
The Penrose process is a known result in black-hole physics. The earlier claim that a civilization can use it for computational energy is not formalized in this volume, and it is withdrawn as a claim of this work.

\section{Entangled-Photon Communication (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that entangled photon streams allow instantaneous interstellar communication conflicts with the no-signaling theorem of quantum mechanics. It is withdrawn. The model contains no such mechanism.

\section{Scope and Open Items}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Not established here: any galactic civilization; energy extraction from black holes; interstellar communication; the structure of real galactic ecosystems.
\end{itemize}
