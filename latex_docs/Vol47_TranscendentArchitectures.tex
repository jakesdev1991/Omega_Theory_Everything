\section*{Layman's Summary}
The Kardashev scale is a speculative classification of civilizations by energy use. This volume records a tier ladder with idealized energy scales. It does not show that any civilization exists at any tier, and it does not describe how physical constants could be changed.

\section{Civilizational Tiers (Formal Result within the Model)}
The energy scales of tiers I to IV are strictly increasing, and tier IV is maximal. These are facts about the ladder as defined.

\section{Type IV Civilizations (Naming Convention, No Physical Result)}
The file's statement that a Type IV civilization exists is the inhabitation of the top tier, kept under its legacy name. It is not a physical result, and it is not evidence for any civilization.

\section{Parameter Tuning (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that fundamental constants can be tuned locally by altering vacuum entropy has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn as a claim of this work and is listed as an open item.

\section{Artificial Universes (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that a civilization can pinch off spacetime and let a new universe inflate has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn.

\section{Scope and Open Items}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Not established here: existence of advanced civilizations; tuning of physical constants; creation of universes; the physical meaning of the tier energy scales.
\end{itemize}
