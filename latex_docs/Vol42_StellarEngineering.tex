\section*{Layman's Summary}
A Dyson swarm is a hypothetical structure of objects around a star that would capture part of its light for energy. The Kardashev scale classifies civilizations by energy use. This volume records bookkeeping for an idealized swarm model. It does not show that such structures are buildable or that the Kardashev categories are natural thresholds.

\section{Kardashev Scale (Definition, Not a Physical Result)}
The Kardashev categories are conventions for energy use. The file does not show that they are physical quanta or thresholds set by quantum limits. The earlier claim is withdrawn.

\section{Idealized Dyson Swarm (Formal Result within the Model)}
In the stacked-shell model, the cumulative area follows the stated recurrence, is monotone, and grows strictly. These are finite algebraic facts about the model's area bookkeeping.

\section{Energy Capture (Formal Result within the Model)}
Captured luminosity is bounded by the total luminosity whenever the capture fraction is at most one. This is an inequality over the reals.

\section{Swarm Optimality and Stability (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that independent orbital nodes are the mathematically optimal solution, balancing gravity and light pressure, has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn as a claim of this work.

\section{Star Lifting (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that magnetic extraction of stellar gas can extend a star's lifetime has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn and is listed as an open item.

\section{Scope and Open Items}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Not established here: feasibility of any stellar-engineering project; optimality of swarm designs; star lifting; the physical meaning of the Kardashev categories.
\end{itemize}
