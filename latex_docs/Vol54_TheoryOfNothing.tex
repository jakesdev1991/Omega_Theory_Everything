\section*{Layman's Summary}
Why is there something rather than nothing? It is one of the oldest questions in philosophy, and it is not answered by mathematics alone. This volume records the formal properties of the empty type, which is the mathematical object for ``no elements''. It does not answer the metaphysical question.

\section{The Empty Type (Formal Result)}
The empty type has no elements. Every predicate holds vacuously on it, and any map out of it is unique. These are standard facts about the empty type.

\section{Legacy Metric Reflexivity (Consistency Check)}

The earlier claim that the axioms of the framework are the unique structure of ``the Void'' has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn.

\section{Symmetry Breaking of the Void (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that the Void spontaneously breaks symmetry into the algebra of the framework has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn.

\section{The Necessity of Existence (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that the universe exists by mathematical necessity, because nothingness is a contradiction, has no formal counterpart. The empty type is not a contradiction. The claim is withdrawn and is listed as an open item. The question of why there is something rather than nothing is not settled by this work.
