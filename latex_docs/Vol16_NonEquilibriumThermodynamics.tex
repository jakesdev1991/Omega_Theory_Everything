\section*{Layman's Summary}
Non-equilibrium thermodynamics studies systems that are driven or changing, such as a dividing cell or an engine. This volume states the basic entropy-balance relations for such a system under explicit assumptions. It does not derive them from microscopic dynamics.

\section{Entropy Balance (Model Assumption and Formal Result)}
The balance law relating entropy, flux, and production is taken as a model assumption. Under isolation at every time, the formal file shows that entropy is nondecreasing. With strictly positive production, the entropy is strictly increasing. Nonnegative production by itself is not enough for an open system, and the file contains an explicit example of an exporting process whose entropy decreases.

\section{Arrow of Time in This Model}
Entropy monotonicity in this model follows from isolation and the sign of production. It does not follow from the framework's modular flow. The formal file does not derive an arrow of time from the underlying dynamics. See Volume 21.

\section{Crooks and Jarzynski Theorems (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claims that the Crooks fluctuation theorem and the Jarzynski equality are proved in this framework have no formal counterpart. They are withdrawn as claims of this work and are listed as open items.
