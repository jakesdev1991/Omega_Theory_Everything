\section*{Layman's Summary}
The Lotka--Volterra equations are a standard model of predator and prey populations. They have a coexistence equilibrium under some conditions, and extinction equilibria otherwise. This volume records those facts for the two-species model. It does not model whole ecosystems.

\section{Predator--Prey Model (Formal Result within the Model)}
The formal file defines the prey and predator rates and establishes the standard equilibrium facts: the equilibrium, its classification, and the existence of a positive coexistence equilibrium under the stated conditions. These are textbook results for this system.

\section{Extinction Equilibrium (Formal Result within the Model)}
The file identifies the extinction equilibrium and classifies it. This is a finite check on the model.

\section{Symbiotic Integration (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that competing networks inevitably merge into cooperative structures has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn as a claim of this work and is listed as an open item.

\section{Trophic Cascades (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that removing an apex predator reorganizes the physical structure of an environment has no formal counterpart. The model has no spatial structure. It is withdrawn.

\section{Keystone Species (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that ecosystems contain topological keystones has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn.

\section{Scope and Open Items}

  \item Not established here: multi-species ecosystems; spatial effects; trophic cascades; keystone species; empirical fit to any ecosystem.
