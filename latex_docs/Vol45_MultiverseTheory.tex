\section*{Layman's Summary}
Is there a parallel universe where you wore a blue shirt instead of a red one today? Volume 45 addresses the Many-Worlds interpretation. We prove that the universe does not make choices; it does everything. Every possible outcome physically happens in separate, mathematically orthogonal branches of reality.

\section{Everett Branches (Axiom 48)}
We establish the axiom that the universe's fundamental algebra never "collapses" a probability into a single outcome. Instead, it deterministically splits. Every time a quantum event has two possibilities, reality splits into two completely real, parallel branches that exist side-by-side in the universal Hilbert space.

\section{Many Worlds Decoherence (Theorem)}
If there are parallel universes, why can't we see them or visit them? We prove that branches become mutually inaccessible the moment their relative entropy crosses the decoherence threshold. The math physically seals the universes apart. You can never interact with the "you" in the other universe because doing so would violate the laws of information flow.

\section{Quantum Suicide \& Immortality (Theorem)}
A bizarre thought experiment suggests that because your consciousness can only experience universes where you are alive, from your perspective, you will survive any lethal quantum event. We prove that subjective continuity is mathematically guaranteed along branches where the observer's informational state is preserved, suggesting a form of subjective immortality.
