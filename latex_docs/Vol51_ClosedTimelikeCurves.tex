\section*{Layman's Summary}
Closed timelike curves are solutions of general relativity in which worldlines loop back in time. Whether they are physically realized is unknown. Novikov's self-consistency principle is a proposed rule for them. This volume records a finite toy model of self-consistency. It does not show that time travel is possible.

\section{Toy Time-Loop Operator (Formal Result within the Model)}
In the three-state chain, the time-loop operator acts idempotently on consistent histories, settles every history to the fixed point, and the fixed point is the only consistent history. These are finite facts about the model.

\section{Novikov Self-Consistency (Formal Result within the Model)}
The file proves self-consistency for the toy operator. It does not prove the physical Novikov principle, which concerns solutions of the Einstein equations.

\section{Chronology Protection (Not Formalized)}
The earlier claim that chronology protection is a thermodynamic limit, and that time usually flows forward because entropy increases, is not formalized here. It is withdrawn as a claim of this work.

\section{Closed Timelike Curves in Spacetime (No Formal Counterpart)}
The earlier claim that negative informational pressure near spinning black holes or traversable wormholes produces closed timelike curves has no formal counterpart. It is withdrawn and is listed as an open item.
